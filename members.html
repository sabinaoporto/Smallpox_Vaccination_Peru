Members of the expedition

Physicians and caretakers

Francisco Xavier [de] Balmis

Director of the Expedition.
Born in a family of surgeons, he worked in the Military Hospital in Alicante and as a military doctor in Mexico, where he lived and researched for a while. He graduated as a surgeon in the Real Protomedicato of Valencia. According to Ramírez Martín (1998), he can be described as an enlightened scientist for the approach he used for the planification of the Expedition (p. 244).

José Salvany Lleopart

Sub director of the Expedition.
He was a surgeon of the Real Sitio de Aranjuez and a graduated from the Real College of Barcelona. His participation on the Expedition was proposed by the surgeons Antonio de Gimbernat Arbós, Leonardo Galli and Ignacio Lacaba.

Manuel Julián Grajales

Hired as helper (ayudante).
He graduated from the Real College of Saint Carlos. His participation on the Expedition was proposed by the surgeons Antonio de Gimbernat Arbós, Leonardo Galli and Ignacio Lacaba. He accompanied Salvany when the Expedition bifurcated in Caracas.

Antonio Gutierrez Robredo

Hired as helper (ayudante).
He served as doctor (médico de número) in the army of Extremadura; he also graduated from the Real College of Saint Carlos. His participation in the Expedition was proposed by Francisco Xavier [de] Balmis.

Francisco Pastor [y] Balmis

Hired as a practitioner (practicante)
Nephew of Balmis, he worked as his helper before the Expedition was conceived. His participation in the Expedition was proposed by his uncle, as he believed his expertise would be beneficial for the task upfront.

Rafael Lozano Pérez

Hired as a practitioner (practicante)
According to the documentation of the Expedition left by Balmis the General Archive of the Indies has, he was a “proved surgeon that had already been practicing the new inoculation”. His participation in the Expedition was proposed by Balmis. When the Expedition bifurcated in Caracas, he was sent along with the group of Salvany.

Basilio Bolaños

Hired as nurse
His participation in the Expedition was proposed by Balmis. He was sent with Salvany when the Expedition bifurcated in Caracas.

Antonio Pastor

Hired as nurse. Nephew of Balmis (Isusi, 2021, pp. 99).

Pedro Ortega

Hired as nurse. His participation in the Expedition was proposed by Balmis. He died while working in the Expedition, in Manila (Isusi, 2021, pp. 99).

Isabel Zendal Gómez

Hired as the children's caretaker.
She was posthumously recognized as the first female nurse in an international mission and was the only woman formally acknowledge to be part of the Expedition since the beginning. Before her participation in the journey to America, she was the Director of the Casa de los Expósitos of Coruña, from where most of the kids of the Expedition were taken from.
